# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

TechCity staff who prepare official documents. They already know which document they need. The job is to produce a certificate, an offer letter, or an appreciation letter without rebuilding the approved layout.

## Product Purpose

Techcity Studio generates those three documents from fixed templates. The template holds letterhead, borders, and approved wording. The person supplies the details that change, then downloads a PDF. Success is a document that matches the template Techcity Studio already uses.

## Positioning

The layout is not assembled in the browser. Each document is filled into an approved template, so the result stays in the form TechCity already issues.

## Operating Context

Staff open the tool, pick a document, fill the fields that document actually has, and download a PDF. Offer letters and appreciation letters can be generated today. Certificate is still coming soon. There is no public signup, pricing, or customer account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Documents: certificate, offer letter, appreciation letter.
- Offer letters support the old and new templates and download as PDF.
- Appreciation letters can be generated and downloaded as PDF.
- Certificate generation is not live yet.
- Landing page sections, confirmed for this surface: navbar, hero, products, how it works, footer.
- The products section is the three documents: certificate, offer letter, and appreciation letter.
- Light appearance only. No dark theme. The landing page uses black ink and gray type on a light ground.
- Do not invent testimonials, customer counts, pricing, or logos of outside organizations.

## Brand Commitments

- Name on the mark: Techcity Studio.
- The logo mark uses the primary color.
- Primary: #4677E2
- Secondary: #F59E0B
- Backgrounds stay light.

## Evidence on Hand

- Wordmark image: `public/techcity-logo.png` (currently black).
- Offer-letter templates and the working editor under `src/app/offer-letter` and `src/lib/offer-letter-pdf.ts`.
- No testimonials, usage metrics, press, or third-party proof. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

- The template is the authority. People change details, not the design of the document.
- Show only the documents and steps the product actually has.
- Say what the tool does in plain language. Staff are here to finish a document, not to be sold a category.
- Keep the interface on a light background with the two committed colors.
